## sms-balik
T I got a text about a parcel and I'm asked to pay a fee
A Messages like this are very often a scam. Scammers want you to enter your payment card details on a link.
1 Don't click the link in the message and don't pay anything.
2 If you really are expecting a parcel, check it yourself – on the courier's official website or by phone on the number from their website.
3 Delete the message. You can also report it as spam.
4 If you've already clicked the link and entered your card, call your bank straight away.
W Delivery companies will not ask for your card details through a link in a text message.
## banka-vola
T Someone from the bank is calling and wants me to transfer money
A Hang up. A bank will never ask you to transfer money to a "safe account", and will never ask for your PIN, password or SMS code.
1 End the call immediately. You don't have to be polite.
2 Don't install anything and don't tell anyone any codes.
3 Call your bank yourself – on the number on the back of your payment card or from the bank's official website.
4 If you have already sent money or given out details, tell the bank straight away and call the Czech Police on 158.
W Scammers can even fake the bank's or police's real number. The "right" number may appear on your screen – it can still be a scam.
## uz-jsem-zadal
T I've already clicked a link or entered my card details
A Don't panic, you can still act. The sooner you call your bank, the better the chance of saving your money.
1 Call your bank straight away on the number on the back of your card or from the bank's official website. Ask them to block the card.
2 If you gave someone access to your phone or computer, turn off the internet (Wi-Fi and mobile data).
3 Change your online banking and email passwords – ideally with help from someone close to you.
4 Report the fraud to the Czech Police on 158.
5 Don't be ashamed. Scammers are professionals and fool young and experienced people too.
## falesny-policista
T A "police officer" is calling and wants me to give them money
A The real police will never ask you for money, cash or a bank transfer. It's a scam.
1 Hang up and don't hand anything over to anyone.
2 Call 158 yourself and say what happened.
3 If someone is standing at your door, don't open it and call 158.
## vnoucatko
T A "grandchild" or "daughter" is writing from a new number and wants money
A This is a common scam. Before you send anything, make sure it really is your relative writing to you.
1 Don't send money or codes.
2 Call your relative on their OLD, known number, or ask another family member.
3 You can ask a question only they know the answer to (e.g. your dog's name).
4 If it turns out to be a scammer, block the number.
## vzdalena-plocha
T Someone wants me to install an app (AnyDesk, TeamViewer)
A Never install an app because someone who called or wrote to you told you to. It would give them access to your phone and your banking.
1 End the call or conversation.
2 Don't install anything. If you already have, turn off the internet and uninstall the app.
3 Call your bank on its official number and tell them what happened.
W Banks, Microsoft and phone operators never call you about "viruses on your computer".
## investice
T Someone is offering me an investment, cryptocurrency or quick profit
A A promise of high, "guaranteed" profit is a typical sign of a scam. Photos of famous people are often misused for it.
1 Don't send anyone money or documents.
2 Don't install any apps an "adviser" recommends.
3 On the Czech National Bank's website you can check whether a company has a licence.
4 Talk about investments in person at your bank or with someone close to you.
W DIGI parťák does not give investment advice – it only helps you recognise a scam.
## vyhra
T I've supposedly won something and have to pay a fee
A If you didn't enter a competition, you haven't won anything. A real prize never requires a fee or your card details.
1 Don't click the link and don't pay anything.
2 Delete the message.
3 If you entered your card, call your bank.
## jak-poznat-podvod
T How do I recognise a scam message or email?
A Scam messages usually share several signs. Noticing just one of them is enough to be careful.
1 Hurry and fear: "immediately", "within 24 hours", "your account will be blocked".
2 A link where you are supposed to fill something in or pay.
3 A request for your card number, PIN, password or SMS code.
4 An unknown sender, a foreign number or a strange address.
5 If you're unsure, paste the message into the "Is it a scam?" tool or ask someone close to you.
## heslo
T How do I create a safe password?
A The best is a long password made of several words that you can easily remember but nobody else can guess.
1 Join 3–4 unrelated words, e.g. "Blackbird-Roll-Garden-27" (but don't use this one, it's public).
2 Use a different password for each account – especially for email and banking.
3 You can write passwords in a notebook that stays at home. Never photograph or send them.
4 Where possible, turn on two-step verification (a code by SMS or in an app).
W Never tell anyone your password – not the bank, not a "technician". Not even DIGI parťák.
## nakup-online
T How do I shop safely online?
A Shop at well-known online stores, and with unknown ones first check who is behind them.
1 Type the shop's address yourself, don't click links from adverts and messages.
2 Look for the seller's contact details, company ID and address, and reviews from other people.
3 Be careful when the price is suspiciously low.
4 With an unknown seller, choose to pay on delivery.
5 On second-hand marketplaces, never send an SMS code or enter your card via a link from a "buyer".
## laska-online
T I met someone online and they want money
A If someone you have never met in person asks you for money, it is very likely a scam.
1 Don't send money, gifts or codes.
2 Talk about it with someone close to you.
3 If you've already sent money, call your bank and the Czech Police (158).

## wifi
T How do I connect to Wi-Fi?

T Should I update my phone?

3 If a link in a text or email asks you to "update", it's a scam.
## screenshot

## facebook
T What is Facebook and how do I start using it?
A Facebook is a social network – a place on the internet where people share photos and news and send each other messages (via the Messenger app). It's free to use.
1 Download the Facebook app from Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iPhone), or type facebook.com into your browser yourself.
2 New account: tap "Create new account" and fill in your name, date of birth and your email or phone number. Choose a password.
3 Logging in: enter your email or phone number and password and tap "Log in".
4 In the privacy settings, choose that your posts are seen only by "Friends".
5 Accept friend requests only from people you really know.
W Facebook has fake profiles and adverts for "guaranteed earnings" featuring famous people. If someone asks you for money, check by phone.
## whatsapp-zacit
T What is WhatsApp and how do I install it?
A WhatsApp is an app for messages, photos and calls (including video) over the internet. On Wi-Fi it's free, even abroad. It uses your phone number.
1 Open Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iPhone) and search for "WhatsApp Messenger".
2 Check that the developer is "WhatsApp LLC" and tap Install (Get).
3 Open WhatsApp, accept the terms and enter your phone number.
4 You'll get a text with a code – enter it only directly in WhatsApp.
5 Type your name. Your contacts who use WhatsApp will appear automatically.
W Never send your WhatsApp verification code to anyone – not even a "friend" who asks for it. That's how scammers steal accounts.
## email
T How do I set up an email address and send a message with an attachment?
A Email is electronic mail. The most popular in the Czech Republic are Seznam email and Google's Gmail. Both are free.
1 Setting up: on seznam.cz tap "E-mail" and "Register", or create a Google account (Gmail) – on Android you usually already have one.
2 Choose an address (e.g. jana.novakova@seznam.cz) and a safe password. Write both down.
3 Sending: tap "Compose", enter the address in the "To" field, write a subject and the text.
4 Attachment (photo): tap the paperclip icon and choose a photo.
5 Tap "Send" (the arrow).
W Emails that want your password, a payment or a click on a link are often scams. If you're unsure, paste them into "Is it a scam?".
## youtube
T What is YouTube and how do I search for videos?
A YouTube is a website and app with videos – songs, programmes, recipes, how-tos. It's free, an advert just plays now and then.
1 Open the YouTube app (usually pre-installed on the phone) or type youtube.com into your browser.
2 Tap the magnifying glass at the top and type what you're looking for (e.g. "Frank Sinatra" or "apple pie recipe").
3 Tap the video. You can skip an advert after a few seconds with the "Skip" button.
4 Turn the phone sideways to make the video fill the screen.
W Adverts for getting rich quickly or miracle cures are often scams, even when famous people appear in them.
## aplikace-instalace
T How do I install an app safely?
A Only download apps from the official store: Google Play on Android, the App Store on iPhone. Most apps are free.
1 Open Google Play (coloured triangle) or the App Store (blue "A").

## zapomenute-heslo
T I've forgotten my password – what now?
A A forgotten password can almost always be reset. The service sends a code to your phone or email and you set a new password.
1 On the login page, tap "Forgot password?".
2 Enter your email or phone number. You'll get a text or email with a code or link.
3 Enter the code only on the page where you started the reset yourself.
4 Choose a new password and write it in a notebook that stays at home.
5 For online banking, call your bank on the number on the back of your card.
W If you receive a password reset code you didn't ask for, don't send it to anyone – someone may be trying to get into your account.
## baterie
T My phone battery runs out quickly – what can I do?
A A bright screen, navigation, videos and apps running in the background use the most energy. A few changes make the battery last much longer.
1 Lower the screen brightness (pull down the bar from the top and move the slider with the sun).
2 Turn on power saving: Android – Settings → Battery → Power saving; iPhone – Settings → Battery → Low Power Mode.
3 Close apps you're not using and turn off location (GPS) when you don't need it.
4 Charge with an original or good-quality charger. You don't have to wait until the phone is completely flat.
5 If the phone is several years old and still drains quickly, a battery replacement at a service centre may help.
## pamet
T My phone says the memory is full – how do I free up space?
A Space on the phone is most often taken up by videos, photos and WhatsApp files. Back them up before deleting them.
1 See what takes up space: Android – Settings → Storage (or Device care); iPhone – Settings → General → iPhone Storage.
2 Back up your photos (Google Photos or iCloud) and then delete large videos.
3 In WhatsApp: Settings → Storage and data → Manage storage – delete large files and forwarded videos.
4 Uninstall apps you don't use.
5 Finally, empty the bin in Photos ("Recently Deleted").
## pomaly-telefon
T My phone is slow or freezes
A A simple restart helps most often. It lets the phone "tidy up" its memory.
1 Restart the phone: hold the power button and choose "Restart" (on iPhone, turn it off and on again).
2 If the phone doesn't respond at all, hold the power button longer (about 10–15 seconds).
3 Install system and app updates.
4 Free up memory – a full memory slows the phone down.
5 If nothing helps, ask at your operator's shop or service centre.
## hlasitost
T I can't hear it ring – how do I make my phone louder?
A You set the ringtone volume with the buttons on the side of the phone or in Settings. Also check the phone isn't in silent mode.
1 Press the volume up button on the side of the phone when no video is playing.
2 Android: Settings → Sounds and vibration → Volume → move "Ringtone" to the right.
3 iPhone: Settings → Sounds & Haptics → move the "Ringtone and Alerts" slider. On the side, check the silent mode switch (orange = silent).
4 Turn off "Do Not Disturb" (the moon icon in the top bar).
5 Turn on vibration too, so you feel it ring in your pocket.

## mobilni-data

## qr-kod

## pin-karty

A You can change your card PIN in your bank's app, in online banking, at a branch or at some of your bank's cash machines.

## hoax
T How do I recognise a hoax or fake news?
A A hoax is an alarming or made-up message that spreads by forwarding. It often frightens, angers or promises miracles.
1 Be alert when a message says "forward to everyone" or is very frightening and outrageous.
2 Look for the source: who wrote it and where was it published? Serious news has an author and a date.
3 Check it with a trustworthy news outlet or on fact-checking websites (in the Czech Republic Manipulátoři.cz and Demagog.cz).
4 If you're not sure, don't forward the message.
## cookies

2 If you don't see such a button, tap "Settings" and leave only "Necessary" turned on.
3 You don't have to deal with the bar every time – the website remembers your choice.

## G screenshot-android
T Screenshot on Android
I A screenshot is a photo of what is on your screen right now. It's useful, for example, for checking a suspicious message.
S Open what you want to capture || For example a suspicious text. Open the message so the whole text and the sender are visible. ||
S Find two buttons || On the side of the phone there's a power button and volume buttons. You'll need the power button and the lower volume button (volume down). ||
S Press both at once || Press the power and volume down buttons at the same time. Hold them only briefly (about 1 second) and let go. || If the power-off menu appears instead, you let go too late. Try again, more quickly.
S The screen flashes || You'll hear a sound or see a brief flash and a small preview at the bottom. The screenshot is saved. ||
S Where to find it || Open the Gallery or Google Photos app. The screenshot is among the newest photos, often in the "Screenshots" folder. ||
S Doesn't work? || On some phones (e.g. Samsung) you can also do it like this: pull down the bar from the top edge and tap "Screenshot". It differs by manufacturer. || You can upload the screenshot in DIGI parťák under "Is it a scam?".
## G screenshot-iphone
T Screenshot on iPhone
I A screenshot is a photo of what is on your screen right now. How you do it depends on whether your iPhone has a round Home button below the screen.
S Open what you want to capture || For example a suspicious text. Open the message so the whole text and the sender are visible. ||
S iPhone WITHOUT a Home button || Newer iPhones (screen to the edge): press the side button on the right and the volume up button on the left (upper) at the same time. Let go straight away. ||
S iPhone WITH a Home button || Older iPhones and iPhone SE (round button at the bottom): press the Home button and the side button on the right at the same time (on the oldest models the top button). ||
S The screen flashes || The screen briefly turns white and a small preview appears in the bottom left. You don't need to tap it – the screenshot saves itself. || If the "slide to power off" slider appears instead, you held the buttons too long. Tap Cancel and try again more quickly.
S Where to find it || Open the Photos app. The screenshot is among the newest photos and also in the "Screenshots" album. || You can upload the screenshot in DIGI parťák under "Is it a scam?".
## G banka-hovor
T The "bank" is calling me – what now?
I The most common scam targeting seniors. Just remember one rule: hang up and call back yourself.
S Hang up || If someone from the "bank" or "police" calls you about money, end the call. You don't need to apologise. ||
S Don't share anything || Never tell anyone your PIN, passwords or SMS codes. A bank will never ask you for them. ||
S Call them yourself || Take your payment card and call the number on the back of it. ||
S Say what happened || The bank employee will tell you whether your account is OK. ||
## G erecept
T Collect an e-prescription (eRecept)
I A prescription no longer has to be on paper. All you need is the code your doctor sends you.
S Wait for the message || Your doctor sends you a text or email with the prescription code. They can also print it for you. ||
S Go to a pharmacy || You can go to any pharmacy. ||
S Show the code || Open the message from your doctor and show it to the pharmacist, or read out the code. ||
S Done || The pharmacist scans the code and gives you the medicine. || You can forward the code to someone close who will collect the medicine for you.

T DIGI parťák on your home screen
I Add your companion to the home screen like an app. Then you open it with one tap.
S Open DIGI parťák in the browser || Android: in Chrome. iPhone: in Safari. ||
S Open the menu || Android: tap the three dots in the top right. iPhone: tap the Share icon at the bottom (a square with an arrow pointing up). ||
S Add to home screen || Choose "Add to Home screen" or "Install app". ||
S Confirm || Tap "Add". An icon with a smiling speech bubble appears on your home screen. ||
